02Context and Problem Statement

TechCorp France is a fast-growing SaaS company of 450 employees. Headcount grew 35% in two years, placing the HR team under significant operational pressure.

The Head of HR requested a consulting engagement after identifying that 60% of HR team time was spent on repetitive, low-value tasks that AI tools could accelerate significantly.

"My team spends hours writing the same job descriptions, rephrasing the same emails, searching for the right phrasing for interviews. ChatGPT is everywhere but nobody knows how to actually use it in our context. I need this to become concrete and applicable from the very next week."

Sophie M. — Head of HR, TechCorp France · Exploratory interview, 15 April 2025

04SMART Objectives
Objective 1 — Adoption (J+30)
85% of the HR team actively uses ChatGPT for at least 3 different daily tasks — measured by weekly self-reporting and dashboard tracking.
Objective 2 — Productivity (J+60)
Each team member saves an average of 1h30 per day on identified tasks. Before/after time comparison.
Objective 3 — Autonomy (J+90)
100% of learners can create their own prompts adapted to their specific use cases without assistance. Practical final assessment.

06Recommendation and Next Steps

A two-phase program: (1) a 4-hour live workshop covering priority use cases, followed by (2) three 45-minute async e-learning modules. Monthly dashboard tracking to measure adoption and adjust if needed.
