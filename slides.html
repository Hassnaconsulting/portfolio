M00 — Introduction
How ChatGPT works — What you need to know
5 min

3 things to understand

1. It predicts, it doesn't know
ChatGPT generates the most statistically likely response. It doesn't look things up — it doesn't "know" your company. You must provide the context.
2. Input quality = output quality
A vague prompt gives a generic answer. A structured prompt with role, context, task, format, and constraints gives an immediately usable result.
3. It doesn't remember previous conversations
Each new session starts fresh. Keep your best prompts in a library — you'll build yours today.
Data security — mandatory rule
Never paste personally identifiable data (names, employee IDs, salary information) into the free version of ChatGPT. Always anonymize before submitting. Check your company's AI policy before using any AI tool with confidential data.
What ChatGPT is great at for HR
Writing and rewriting · Structuring information · Generating questions · Summarizing long documents · Adapting tone for different audiences · Creating templates

Use case 2 — STAR behavioral interview questions
15 min

What is the STAR method?

S — Situation
The context or challenge
T — Task
Their specific responsibility
A — Action
What they personally did
R — Result
Measurable outcome
Prompt — STAR questions
You are an expert recruiter.
Generate 10 behavioral STAR questions to evaluate [COMPETENCY] for a [JOB TITLE] role.
For each question: state the exact competency assessed and the positive signal expected in the answer.
Why this is powerful
You get not only the questions — you also get the implicit evaluation rubric. You know exactly what to listen for in each answer. Preparation time: 90 seconds instead of 25 minutes.
Example output
"Describe a situation where two managers in your scope had conflicting objectives. What was your role, what did you do, and what was the outcome?"
Signal: ability to navigate conflict without taking sides · outcome focus

M03 — Performance
Use case 9 — Giving difficult feedback constructively
10 min

When direct feedback needs to land well

ChatGPT can help you find the right words — firm, clear, and constructive — without losing the relationship.

Prompt — Constructive feedback formulation
Help me formulate feedback for a team member about [SITUATION / BEHAVIOR].
The impact has been: [DESCRIBE IMPACT]
I want the feedback to be: direct but not harsh · specific not vague · focused on behavior not personality.
Suggest 3 different ways to open this conversation.
SBI Framework — Structure to follow
S — Situation: "During the team meeting on Tuesday…"
B — Behavior: "When you interrupted the presenter three times…"
I — Impact: "It created tension and reduced her confidence to continue."

ChatGPT can generate SBI-structured feedback from your bullet points.
Important limit
ChatGPT formulates — you deliver. Never paste the AI output verbatim into a performance review without personalizing it. Your judgment and knowledge of the person are irreplaceable.

M04 — Prompt Engineering
5 advanced prompt structures
15 min
1. Chain-of-thought
Ask ChatGPT to reason step by step before answering. Example: "Before answering, list the 3 key constraints, then propose a solution." Useful for complex analysis tasks.
2. Few-shot
Give 2–3 examples of the style or format you want. ChatGPT will match your tone exactly. Paste one of your best past emails as a style reference.
3. Role-play
Simulate difficult scenarios before they happen. "Act as a candidate who resists feedback. I will practice my response." Or: "Play devil's advocate on this job description."
4. Negative constraints
Define what you do NOT want — it's often more effective than specifying what you want. "Do not use HR jargon. Do not use passive voice. Do not exceed 150 words."
5. Iteration without rewriting
After a disappointing output — refine without starting over.
Iteration template
Your output doesn't match because [REASON].
Revise it by: [INSTRUCTION 1], [INSTRUCTION 2].
Keep [WHAT WORKED].
Format: [FORMAT].
