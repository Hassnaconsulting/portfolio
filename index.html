Framework 01
Prosci ADKAR — The human side of change
Resistance, fear of job loss, and low trust are the real adoption barriers. Before any tool training, I map resistance at the individual level using the five ADKAR dimensions and design targeted interventions for each barrier point. Bloom's taxonomy is then applied in Phase D to structure measurable learning objectives — from recall (M00) to evaluation and creation (M04).
Awareness · Desire · Knowledge · Ability · Reinforcement
Framework 02
ADDIE — Structured, measurable learning programs
Once the human conditions are in place, ADDIE structures the learning experience with modular content, H5P e-learning assets exportable in SCORM to any LMS, and measurable outcomes at each stage. Every program tracks real behavior change — not just training completion rates.

About
