03Module Descriptions
Module 00 · Introduction — Why AI is changing HR now (15 min)
Bloom 1–2
Content
Time data shock · Live demo: full job description in 4 min · The 3-check rule: Accuracy · Relevance · Tone
Exercise
Each participant calculates their own recoverable time per week
Module 01 · Recruiting — From job description to candidate summary (55 min)
Bloom 3
Use cases (3)
UC1 — Job description (Slide 6 · 12 min): 45 min → 5 min · Live demo + Exercise 1A
UC2 — STAR interview questions (Slide 7 · 15 min): 10 behavioral questions in 90 sec · STAR method explained · Exercise 1B
UC3 — CV summary and candidate profile (Slide 8 · 12 min): 35 min → 5 min · Privacy rules · H5P Quiz assessment
Key prompt
You are an expert HR professional. Write a complete job description for a [JOB TITLE] at a [SECTOR] company of [SIZE]. Structure: main mission (2 sentences), responsibilities (exactly 5), profile required, benefits. Tone: attractive and inclusive. Max 300 words. Avoid technical HR jargon.
Assessment
H5P quiz · 5 questions · Minimum pass: 4/5  ·  → Try the quiz
Module 02 · Onboarding and Communication — 10 minutes instead of 3 hours (55 min)
Bloom 3–4
Use cases (3)
UC4 — 90-day onboarding plan (Slide 10 · 18 min): 2h30 → 20 min · Personalization technique · Exercise 2A
UC5 — Internal HR emails (Slide 11 · 15 min): tone adaptation technique · company voice matching
UC6 — Document summary (Slide 11 · included): 45 min → 5 min · 3 key points for managers · 1 Slack sentence
Key prompt
Create a 90-day integration plan for [NAME], [JOB TITLE] starting on [DATE]. Company: [SECTOR]. Manager: [NAME]. Organize in 3 phases (30/60/90 days) with: objectives, concrete actions, key contacts, expected deliverables at each stage.
Assessment
Self-assessment checklist · 8 criteria · Minimum 6/8  ·  → Download checklist
Quiz
H5P quiz · 5 questions · Minimum pass 4/5  ·  → Try the quiz
Module 03 · Performance and Appraisals — Prepare, conduct, synthesize (55 min)
Bloom 4–6
Use cases (3)
UC7 — Annual appraisal preparation (Slide 14 · 15 min): 1h15 → 15 min · 5 questions + meeting structure + IDP · Exercise 3A
UC8 — 360° feedback synthesis (Slide 15 · 20 min): 2h → 3 min · Ethics mandatory: anonymize first · Exercise 3B (graded)
UC9 — Difficult feedback — SBI framework (Slide 16 · 10 min): Situation–Behavior–Impact · AI formulates, you deliver
Key prompt
Here are [N] raw 360° feedbacks for [NAME]: [FEEDBACKS]. Synthesize as: 3 recurring strengths, 2 priority improvement areas, 1 watch point, recommended 3-step action plan. Format: structured professional report.
Ethics note
Never paste personally identifiable data into the free version of ChatGPT. Anonymize names before submitting any feedbacks.
Assessment
Role-play scenario evaluated on 4 criteria: relevance, accuracy, ethics, immediate usability
Module 04 · HR Prompt Engineering — Master it, own it (40 min)
Bloom 5–6
Objectives
Design advanced prompts for any HR task · Build a personal prompt library · Detect and correct hallucinations
5 advanced structures
Chain-of-thought · Few-shot · Role-play · Negative constraints · Iteration
Final exercise
Each participant creates 3 original prompts for their 3 most frequent HR tasks — shared in a team Notion library built live during the session
Assessment
30-day adoption plan: 3 AI habits to implement starting Monday
